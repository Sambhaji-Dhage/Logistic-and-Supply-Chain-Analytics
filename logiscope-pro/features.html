Product Features

Automate the entire analyst workflow for logistics and supply chain teams.

Market Data Collection

  • Integrates with logistics APIs, trade portals, and freight indexes
  • Tracks global air/sea freight rates and shipping demand trends
  • Collects competitor pricing and service offerings

Competitor & Industry Analysis

  • Benchmarks company performance vs. competitors
  • Identifies market leaders and emerging logistics hubs
  • Monitors market share and customer sentiment from public data

Customer & Client Research

  • Analyzes shipment behavior and retention rates
  • Identifies high-value customers and underserved routes
  • Segments clients by region, shipment type, and revenue potential

Pricing & Cost Optimization

  • Breaks down cost structures and surcharges
  • Suggests optimal pricing based on demand and competition
  • Forecasts price changes with historical trends

Demand Forecasting & Trends

  • Predicts demand by route, season, and commodity
  • Identifies peak seasons and bottlenecks
  • Generates early alerts for demand fluctuations

Operational Performance Analytics

  • Tracks on-time rate, shipment duration, cost per delivery
  • Detects route and supplier inefficiencies
  • Recommends process improvements from trends

Supplier & Partner Evaluation

  • Evaluates reliability, cost, and delivery time
  • Compares multiple vendors automatically
  • Flags risk of delays or cost overruns

Visualization & Reporting

  • Dynamic dashboards similar to Power BI/Tableau
  • Automated executive reports with exports
  • PDF and Excel downloads for internal teams

Market Opportunity Mapping

  • Highlights new routes, countries, and regions
  • Recommends expansion areas with rising demand
  • Shows real-time logistics hotspots

Risk & Compliance Monitoring

  • Tracks international trade regulation changes
  • Alerts on fuel, customs, and tax policy impacts
  • Evaluates risk factors affecting time and cost

Sentiment & Feedback Analysis

  • Collects and analyzes client feedback
  • Detects satisfaction levels with NLP
  • Suggests service improvements

Automated Stakeholder Reporting

  • Weekly and monthly executive summaries
  • Branch, country, and route comparisons
  • Email and dashboard alerts
